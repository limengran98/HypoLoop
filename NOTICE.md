# Attribution

The HypoLoop implementation is distributed under the MIT License.

The manuscript, result figures, and case illustrations accompany the research
paper **HypoLoop: Revising Weight-Editing Plans from Scientific Feedback**.
When reusing these research materials, please cite the paper and the
corresponding dataset and predictor publications.

Upstream datasets, pretrained models, and their software retain their original
licenses. The [task catalog](docs/DATASETS.md) links the original sources and
publications. Backbone source and model weights are obtained from those
upstream projects.

The numerical implementation uses PyTorch, NumPy, and SciPy. The scientific
tasks build on GEARS, Uni-Mol, CGCNN, PDEBench/FNO, PBT/BatteryLife, LTSF-Linear,
Depth Anything V2, SeisBench/PhaseNet, DC3, AnomalyCLIP, and ChangeMamba.
